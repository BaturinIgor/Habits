## Description
ZenHab helps you build good habits and keep them, one calm day at a time.

Add a habit in seconds, choose the days and a reminder time, and check it off right from the notification or a Home Screen widget. Your streaks, calendar and charts show how far you've come.

HABITS YOUR WAY
• Icon, color, description and groups for every habit
• Flexible schedule: chosen weekdays, with start and end dates or forever, or a single day
• Morning, afternoon, evening and anytime sections, or one list sorted by time
• A catalog of about 50 ready-made habits, added in one tap
• Notes for any check-in

REMINDERS THAT WORK
• A reminder at the time you pick
• If you haven't checked a habit off, the reminder repeats every 15 minutes
• Done and Skip buttons right in the notification stop the repeats

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Current and best streaks, completion rate
• Calendar, charts and an activity heatmap
• Achievements for streaks, perfect days and weeks

STAY MOTIVATED
• A pet hatches from an egg and grows with every habit you check off
• Earn sparks for check-ins and spend them on new pets, avatars, backgrounds and streak freezes
• Confetti when the whole day is done

LEARN WORDS EVERY DAY
• 500 common words in 14 languages
• Flashcards with pronunciation and spaced repetition
• Beginner, intermediate and advanced levels

WIDGETS
• Home Screen widgets: check off habits without opening the app
• Lock Screen widgets with today's progress

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• All your data stays on your iPhone
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Export and import a backup whenever you want

ZENHAB PRO
The free version includes up to 5 active habits. Pro adds:
• Unlimited habits
• Custom reminder repeats
• Daily goals: count glasses of water, pages or minutes
• Pause a habit for a trip or illness without losing your streak
• Exclusive pets (dragon, unicorn, phoenix), a monthly gift of 100 sparks and a higher daily limit
• Exclusive avatars and backgrounds

Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in Settings, Apple Account, Subscriptions.
